## Nascol

Nasaco has a wide range of collectors for all types of flotation. Our expertise in custom formulation has allowed significant improvements in metallurgy to be made by many of our customers when Nasaco formulations are used.

Our collectors are used in the following mineral flotation applications:

- Base and precious metals
- Industrial minerals
- Coal
