## Modifiers

A modifier acts on the mineral surface to either activate the surface and thereby promote flotation or to deactivate the surface to make the mineral non-flotable. Nasaco supplies modifiers made from the purest raw materials which give a superior economic advantage.

The Nasaco range of modifiers:

- Copper Sulphate
- Zinc Sulphate
- Sodium Bichromate
- Sodium Sulphide
- Sodium Hydrogen Sulphide
- Sodium Metabisulphite
- Ferrous Sulphate
