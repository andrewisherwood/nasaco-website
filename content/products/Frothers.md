## Nasfroth

Frothers are an essential reagent in most flotation reagent suites and directly through their action improve grade, recovery or both in a float circuit.

Nasaco is a major supplier of frothers to the mining industry. The NASFROTH™ range of alcohol and glycol frothers includes formulations and blends which give application specific products. These are often developed toghether with our customers. To extend this range, Nasaco is the exclusive distributor of Dowfroth products for Dow Chemical company in the EMEA area. DOWFROTH™ incorporate a range of glycol and glycol ether frothers.

We develop our formulated frothers together with our customers in our R+D labs and on customer sites. By working together with our customers in this way we can offer specifically designed products for their processes.

### Our Chemistries include:

- Alcohols
- Glycols
- Glycol ethers
- Proprietary formulations
