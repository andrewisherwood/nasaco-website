This notice explains what personal data Nasaco International Ltd collects through this website, why, and what your rights are.

## Who we are

Nasaco International Ltd, CH-1304 Cossonay-Ville, Switzerland, is responsible for the personal data collected on this website. If you have any questions about this notice or your data, contact us at info@nasaco.ch or +41 21 863 03 10.

## What we collect and why

**Contact form.** When you send us an enquiry, we collect your name, email address, any other details you enter, and your message. We use them only to reply to your enquiry and to follow up on it.

**Data sheet downloads.** Before your first download, we ask for your email address. We use it to keep a record of which products you are interested in and, where relevant, to contact you about them. We do not send newsletters or sell your address to anyone.

**Technical data.** Like any website, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. This is used only to run and secure the website.

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies, and we do not track you across other websites.

## Who processes your data

Our website and its forms are hosted by Netlify, Inc. (USA). Form submissions are stored by Netlify and sent to us by email. Netlify acts on our behalf and is not allowed to use your data for its own purposes. Because Netlify is based in the United States, your data may be transferred there. These transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses.

We do not share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and download records for as long as needed to deal with your request and maintain our business relationship, and for no more than three years after our last contact with you, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

## Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

- ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it
- have incorrect data corrected
- have your data deleted
- object to our use of your data, including being contacted about products
- withdraw any consent you have given

To exercise any of these rights, email info@nasaco.ch. You also have the right to complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, if you are in the EU, to your local data protection authority.

## Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
