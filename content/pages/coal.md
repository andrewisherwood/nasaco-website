## Coal

Nasaco has developed various "Add Pack" (AP) flotation reagents specifically designed for the flotation of fine coal, in order to increase the overall yield and reduce ash in coal washeries where coal flotation is used.

Beneficial side effects of using Nascol AP products may be found in the reduction in hydrocarbon and frother dosage, and also a reduction in final concentrate filter cake moisture.
