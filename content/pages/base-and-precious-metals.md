## Base and precious metals

Nascol collectors are used in the flotation of:

- **Base metals:** copper, lead, zinc and nickel
- **Precious metals:** gold, silver and platinum group metals (PGMs)

## The Nascol range

The Nascol base and precious metal collector range includes:

- Xanthates
- Xanthic esters
- Xanthogen formates
- Dithiophosphates
- Thionocarbamates
- Mercaptobenzothiazoles
- Mercaptans
- Proprietary products and formulations

The individual Nascol products, with their [data sheets](/collectors/#data-sheets), are listed on the [Collectors](/collectors/) page.
